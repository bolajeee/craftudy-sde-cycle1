## Code practiced

**Two Sum**
- Time: `O(n)`
- Auxiliary space: `O(n)`

**Group Anagrams**
- Time: `O(nk log k)`
- Auxiliary space: `O(nk)`

**Top K Frequent Elements**
- Time: `O(n + m log m)`
- Auxiliary space: `O(m)`

Where `n` is the input size, `k` is the maximum word length for Group Anagrams, and `m` is the number of distinct values for Top K Frequent Elements. Revisit these complexities for a clearer understanding.
